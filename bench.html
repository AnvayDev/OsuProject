Latency and rendering benchmark

Measures, on this machine and browser: what the display and GPU do for each candidate renderer, how late input events reach the page, and how accurate the audio clocks are. It takes about two minutes. Nothing is uploaded: the result is JSON to copy.

Before you start: use the window size you play at, close other heavy tabs, and connect the headphones you play with. Twice during the run you will be asked to move the mouse and tap Z and X as if you were playing, for eight seconds each time.
