## 2. Tests and where their expected values come from

All expected values are the client's own, from its test suite at the pinned tag. Nothing is derived from memory of how osu! behaves.

- `tests/unit/game/OsuBeatmapConversion.test.ts`: `OsuBeatmapConversionTest.cs`. 14 beatmaps (four of them full ranked maps) are converted
  through `getPlayableBeatmap` and every object's start time, end time and stacked position is compared with
  `osu.Game.Rulesets.Osu.Tests/Resources/Testing/Beatmaps/*-expected-conversion.json` (copied to `tests/fixtures/osu-tests/osu-ruleset/`).
  The client's test allows a lenience of 2 because the expected files were generated by osu-stable, which truncates times to whole
  milliseconds; measured against them the port is off by at most 1.0 ms and 0.33 px.
- `tests/unit/game/ScoreProcessor.test.ts`: `osu.Game.Tests/Rulesets/Scoring/ScoreProcessorTest.cs`, with its expected scores in both
  scoring modes (a single Meh on a one-circle map is 83,398 standardised and 8,343 classic), combo accounting and reverting, grades and
  silver grades, and the score multiplier taken from the difficulty before mods.
- `tests/unit/game/OsuHealthProcessor.test.ts`: `OsuHealthProcessorTest.cs` (which objects' minimum result fails at near-zero health), and
  the hit windows of `OsuHitWindows.cs` at OD 0, 5, 8 and 10.
- `tests/unit/game/OsuScoreMultiplier.test.ts`: `OsuScoreMultiplierTest.cs`. Its table of 147 mod combinations is converted mechanically
  from the C# file, plus Difficulty Adjust, the Classic multiplier by total score version, and the default of 1.

## 3. Facts that are easy to get wrong

- **Float arithmetic is reproduced where the client uses `float`.** `OsuHitObject.Scale` (`LegacyRulesetExtensions.cs`
  `CalculateScaleFromCircleSize`: the subtraction is in doubles, the halving and the 1.00041 fudge in floats), `StackOffset`
  (`StackHeight * Scale * -6.4f`), positions (`Vector2F`), the spinner tick times (`(float)(i + 1) / totalSpins`), the stack threshold
  (`(int)TimePreempt * StackLeniency`). Use `Math.fround` in the same places when porting further.
- **Hit windows** are `floor(DifficultyRange(OD, range)) - 0.5` for Great, Ok and Meh, and a fixed 400 ms for Miss
  (`OsuHitWindows.cs:36-41`). At OD 8: 31.5, 75.5, 119.5.
- **A slider is not judged as one object** unless `ClassicSliderBehaviour` is set: its own judgement is `OsuIgnoreJudgement`, the head is a
  full hit circle (`OsuJudgement`), and the tail's `TailJudgement` (`SliderTailHit`, minimum `IgnoreMiss`) carries the final combo
  (`Slider.cs:286`, `SliderTailCircle.cs`). Ticks and repeats are `LargeTickHit`.
- **The legacy last tick** (36 ms before the end) is generated by `SliderEventGenerator` but `Slider` creates no object for it.
- **Stacking** has two algorithms: beatmap version 6 and later (`applyStacking`), and the older one (`applyStackingOld`). The version is
  the `.osu` header's, so tests must decode through `Decoder.getDecoder`, not `new LegacyBeatmapDecoder()` (which assumes the latest version).
- **Score** is `500000 * accuracy * comboProgress + 500000 * accuracy^5 * accuracyProgress + bonus`, rounded, then multiplied by the mod
  multiplier and rounded again (`ScoreProcessor.cs` `ComputeTotalScore`, `updateScore`). Combo score per judgement is
  `base(maxResult) * comboAfter^0.5`. The maximum values come from simulating a perfect play in `ApplyBeatmap`.
- **The mod multiplier depends on the difficulty before mods** (`Beatmap.BeatmapInfo.Difficulty`), not the playable difficulty.
  `BeatmapConverter.Convert` clones the `BeatmapInfo` so the two stay separate.
- **`ScoreMultiplierCalculator` keys multipliers by exact type**, as C# does with `mod.GetType()`: a subclass does not inherit its base
  mod's multiplier. A combination of two mods replaces their single multipliers.
- **osu! grades**: a miss caps S and SS at A (`OsuScoreProcessor.RankFromScore`).
- **Health**: `OsuHealthProcessor` has its own per-result increases (Great 0.03, Ok 0.011, Meh 0.002, Miss −0.03/−0.125/−0.2 at HP 0/5/10)
  and adds a combo-end bonus (0.07, 0.05 or 0.03 by the combo's worst result). The drain rate is found by simulating a perfect play
  (`DrainingHealthProcessor.ComputeDrainRate`), targeting a minimum health of 0.99/0.9/0.4 at HP 0/5/10.

## 4. Web deviations

- **Ruleset instantiation.** C# creates a ruleset by reflection from `RulesetInfo.InstantiationInfo`. Here a ruleset's module registers
  itself (`registerRuleset` in `rulesets/RulesetInfo.ts`), and `OsuGameBase` imports the ported rulesets' modules. This keeps the model
  from importing the implementation, which would be an import cycle through the beatmap classes. A test that calls
  `RulesetInfo.createInstance()` must import `@game/rulesets/osu/OsuRuleset`.
- **`obj is T` in `BeatmapConverter`** becomes the abstract `isConverted(obj)`.
- **Cancellation tokens** are dropped (conversion runs to completion on the main thread).
- **Processors are `Component`s**, as in C#. They only need a clock for `HasCompleted` and the health drain.
